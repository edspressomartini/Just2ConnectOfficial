Harpenden is one of Hertfordshire's premier towns. It has an affluent demographic,
great connectivity to London and many boutique commercial enterprises.
Nonetheless, it still has need for fast broadband and resilient digital telephony.
This is where Just2Connect is playing a significant role in upgrading both broadband
and telephony infrastructure.
Just2Connect acts as a 'whole of market' supplier, meaning that we can take the
best from the many broadband infrastructure providers, including Openreach and
CityFibre, to deliver the fastest and most cost-effective service to businesses.

We have recently been upgrading clients from the traditional FTTC broadband that
uses old copper line infrastructure, to the latest Full Fibre (FTTP) service.
Typically, we have already moved these clients across to digital telephony, so it has
been straight forward to migrate their broadband to FTTP.
One of the other factors that is often neglected when moving to digital telephony is
that handsets need to be connected to the internet - this can be done via ethernet
cabling or WiFi.
Just2Connect has years of experience providing structured cabling in offices -
whether this is just a couple of ethernet points or a complete digital fit-out. This
enables us to seamlessly move clients from the old to the new technologies.
Harpenden Golf Club are a prime example of how we can assist clients in migrating
to the new technological landscape. Whilst they were expanding the clubhouse, we
installed ethernet cabling to all parts of the building to enable us to move them
across to digital telephony at the same time - they were years ahead of the game!
