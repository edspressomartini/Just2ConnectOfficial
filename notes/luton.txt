Luton has areas that aren’t well served by broadband, but all areas have total access
to digital telephony.
One of our clients, High Flying Food, on the periphery of Luton Airport is surprisingly
poorly served by broadband. They can only get the equivalent of standard ADSL2+
(now called SOTAP) – no FTTC or FTTP.
They need both digital telephony connection and good broadband. To solve their
issue, we have installed a Starlink satellite system. It is remarkably easy to install
and monthly subscription costs are cost effective. Broadband speed is approx.
250Mbps – so very fast.
Digital telephony needs broadband that has low latency (below 100ms). Starlink is
able to deliver on this metric and provides a great solution.
Another client, Chiltern Doors, recently wanted to move offices and migrate their
digital telephone numbers with them. This is one of the unexpected benefits of such
telephony, as they are location independent. After we had provisioned broadband
into their new offices, they just had to unplug their phones, take them to the new
showroom, and just plug them in – they were back up and running immediately.
We are within 20 minutes or less of Luton and if our telephony support can’t solve
the problem, it is quick and easy for us to get to the clients site.
