Hemel Hempstead is currently experiencing a major change in it telecommunications
landscape, with the change to digital phone services and the installation of full fibre
internet services.
However, the latest industry metrics show a low take-up of these services.
Openreach estimate that they can reach 77% of properties with their FTTP
infrastructure.
There have been a number of initiatives from BT Openreach and Dacorum Borough
Council to host roadshows and forums to explain what businesses need to do to
make themselves ready for the digital switchover.
There are a number of trends within businesses that readily lend themselves to the
adoption of digital telephony.
 Integrations of the business phone system with VCRM systems
 Integration of AI into the phone system to handle calls
 Accommodating working from home, so that all employees whether in the
office or at home remain part of the office phone system
