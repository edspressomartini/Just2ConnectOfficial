Watford is a large town with a range of requirements for both telephony and
broadband.
Full fibre broadband (FTTP) is widely available in Watford and a number of new
providers have moved into the area.
What do your area or the building you are in is poorly served by broadband?
We have a number of clients in a hub in Watford, who have that very problem. We
have solved the problem by installing SIM enabled routers to provide both
broadband and digital telephony connectivity. Data services in the area are very
good and so the SIM routers are able to cope with the demands of the digital age!
Plus, the cost is relatively low in comparison to standard broadband products and we
are able to provide them on a 30 day contract basis to provide the clients as much
flexibility as possible.
When required, we are able to visit client sites within 20 minutes.
Our flexible contracts include on-site visits at no extra charge, so clients can budget
for their services without any surprise invoice or unexpected costs.
