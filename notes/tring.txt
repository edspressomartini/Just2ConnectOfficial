Tring is a mixture of many local business hubs. An Independent high street, a
number of business parks and plenty of artisan food and drink producers.
All of these businesses need to transition to digital telephony and to support that they
must have reliable broadband connections.
We have already migrated all of our clients in Tring to the new digital services. This
was done in a structured fashion that meant that at no time were they disrupted or
had broadband or telephony outages.
The benefit of using a local provider like Just2Connect for telecoms and broadband
services is that we are able to provide a single invoice for all services. We are local
and can be with clients within 10 minutes and finally, we have proactive account
management.
For example, choosing whether to include all call costs in their telephony tariff or
‘Pay as you Go’ is something that we automatically calculate for all of our clients. We
will look at usage over the previous 12 months and calculate which tariff is the best
for them.
